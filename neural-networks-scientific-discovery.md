# Neural networks for scientific discovery: hard-won insights

[Slides (PDF)](slides/alexmatthews_neural_networks_scientific_discovery.pdf) ·
[Interactive slides](slides/alexmatthews_neural_networks_scientific_discovery/index.html)

Abstract: What does it take for neural networks to contribute to genuine scientific discovery? Drawing on three projects, Fermionic Neural Networks for the many-electron Schrödinger equation, the discovery of unstable self-similar singularities in fluid equations, and materials discovery at CuspAI, this talk shares some hard-won lessons. A recurring theme is that good methods are necessary but not sufficient: you also need to be "on it" to discover things.
